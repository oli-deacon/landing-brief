# Goa content sources

Destination: Goa, India. Arrival airport: Goa International Airport, Dabolim (GOI).
Updated 2 October 2026. Transfer times are planning estimates, not a timetable or fare guarantee.

- [AAI airport information](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/goa) and [AAI airport designation](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip-v2-01-2025/pdf/Official%20AIC/AIC2023/AIC14_2023.pdf): Dabolim / GOI.
- [Goa Tourism transport](https://goa-tourism.com/getting-around/) and [GoaMiles](https://goa-tourism.com/goa-miles-passenger/): prepaid taxis and local app bookings.
- [Indian Visa Online](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/) and [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/india): entry guidance; e-Arrival Card is separate from a visa.
- [Fontainhas](https://goa-tourism.com/popular-destination/experience-fontainhas/), [beaches](https://goa-tourism.com/beach/) and [Colva](https://www.goa.gov.in/places/colva-beach/): Explore locations and suggested running starting points. Running suggestions are flexible route-finding outings, not surveyed courses.
- [Goan dishes](https://goa-tourism.com/food/know-your-favourite-dishes/) and [sweets](https://goa-tourism.com/food/goan-sweets/): food notes.
- [Languages of Goa](https://dip.goa.gov.in/languages-of-goa/): local language context. Existing Hindi travel phrases are explicitly labelled as Hindi.

Atlas coordinates identify Dabolim airport, the arrival point for this destination.
